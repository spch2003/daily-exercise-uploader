The base of a solid right prism is a regular $6$-sided polygon of side $8$ cm. If the volume of the prism is $288 \text{ cm}^3$, find the total surface area of the prism correct to the nearest $\text{cm}^2$.
A. $166 \text{ cm}^2$
B. $249 \text{ cm}^2$
C. $416 \text{ cm}^2$
D. $748 \text{ cm}^2$ || To find the total surface area, we calculate the area of the base and the lateral surface area.
The base is a regular $6$-sided polygon. We divide it into $6$ congruent triangles from the center.
[TIKZ]
\begin{tikzpicture}
\draw (-2,0) -- (2,0) -- (0, 3.46) -- cycle;
\draw[dashed] (0, 3.46) -- (0,0);
\node[below] at (-1,0) {$4$};
\node[below] at (1,0) {$4$};
\node[right] at (0, 1.73) {$h_a$};
\draw (0, 2.96) arc (270:300:0.5);
\node[right] at (0.1, 2.76) {$30^\circ$};
\end{tikzpicture}
[/TIKZ]
The central angle is $360^\circ / 6 = 60^\circ$, so the half-angle is $30^\circ$.
The height of the triangle (apothem) is $h_a = 4 / \tan(30^\circ) = 6.928$ cm.
Area of one triangle = $1/2 \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = 1/2 \times 8 \times 6.928 = 27.712 \text{ cm}^2$.
Area of base = $6 \times 27.712 = 166.27 \text{ cm}^2$.
Volume = Base Area $\times$ Prism Height ($H$), so $288 = 166.27 \times H$, giving $H = 1.732$ cm.
Lateral Area = Perimeter $\times H = (6 \times 8) \times 1.732 = 83.14 \text{ cm}^2$.
Total Surface Area = $2 \times 166.27 + 83.14 = 415.68 \text{ cm}^2$, nearest to $416 \text{ cm}^2$. || C
