# Pet Outlook Design (Cute School Mascot)

## Visual Direction
- Style: chibi, toy-like, soft and round
- Feel: friendly, playful, not too flashy
- Finish: matte body + glossy eyes

## Core Palette
- Fur main: #F4C95D
- Fur shadow: #D89B3D
- Inner ear: #F7B6C2
- Eye iris: #2D6AE3
- Eye highlight: #FFFFFF
- Nose: #5B3A29
- Paw pad: #EFA8A8

## Mood Accent Colors
- streak_broken (sad): accent #7A8AA0
- streak_3d (little happy): accent #53B37B
- streak_7d (happy): accent #30A46C
- streak_14d (very happy): accent #F2B94B

## Expression + Pose Guide
- streak_broken:
  - ears slightly down
  - tail low
  - body slight forward slouch
  - idle speed slow
- streak_3d:
  - tiny smile
  - gentle upright stance
  - tail neutral raised
- streak_7d:
  - wider smile
  - chest slightly up
  - subtle bounce idle
- streak_14d:
  - biggest smile
  - energetic bounce idle
  - confident heroic chest-up pose

## Animation recommendation (free via Mixamo)
- streak_broken: idle/sad idle (slow)
- streak_3d: idle relaxed
- streak_7d: happy idle
- streak_14d: victory idle / celebration idle
