The length of a side of a solid cube is $60 \text{ cm}$. The volume of a solid right circular cylinder is equal to the volume of the cube while the curved surface area of the circular cylinder is equal to the total surface area of the cube. Find the height of the circular cylinder.
A. $\frac{270}{\pi} \text{ cm}$
B. $\frac{540}{\pi} \text{ cm}$
C. $\frac{1080}{\pi} \text{ cm}$
D. $\frac{2160}{\pi} \text{ cm}$
||
Volume of cube $= 60^3 = 216000 \text{ cm}^3$
Total surface area of cube $= 6 \times 60^2 = 21600 \text{ cm}^2$

Let $r$ and $h$ be the base radius and height of the cylinder respectively.
$\pi r^2 h = 216000$  --- (1)
$2\pi r h = 21600$    --- (2)

Divide (1) by (2):
$\frac{\pi r^2 h}{2\pi r h} = \frac{216000}{21600} \implies \frac{r}{2} = 10 \implies r = 20 \text{ cm}$

Substitute $r = 20$ into (2):
$2\pi (20) h = 21600$
$40\pi h = 21600$
$h = \frac{21600}{40\pi}$
$h = \frac{540}{\pi} \text{ cm}$
